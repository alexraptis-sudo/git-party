Alexandros Raptis is a student of Devine, the Digital Experience Design & Development program, where design thinking and web development come together. They are drawn to interfaces that feel alive: interactions that respond, surprise and invite people to play.

Within Creative Code Kortrijk, Alexandros enjoys turning small experiments into polished experiences, using code as a creative medium rather than only a tool. Version control, teamwork and a lot of iteration are part of how they work.

Their goal is to keep learning by making things, and to share what they discover with the people around them.
