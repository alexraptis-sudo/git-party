Riccardo Rossi is a student of Devine, the Digital Experience Design & Development program, where creativity and technology are studied side by side. They are curious about how code can give digital products personality and make them feel more human.

At Creative Code Kortrijk, Riccardo likes to experiment with generative visuals, motion and new tools, and to see how far a simple idea can be pushed. They value collaboration, and enjoy learning from the different backgrounds of the people they build with.

Riccardo wants to grow into a developer who combines technical craft with a designer's eye.
